# ProfitGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-26

## 1. Who we are

ProfitGuard ("we", "us", "our") provides analytics and alerting for Shopify merchants to help detect potential profit leaks.

Business name: **ZesToraX**  
Contact email: **support@zestorax.com**  
Website: **https://zestorax.com**

## 2. Scope

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when merchants install and use the ProfitGuard Shopify app.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Merchant account and shop information
- Shop domain and Shopify shop identifiers
- App installation and subscription status
- Session/authentication metadata required to operate the app

### 3.2 Shopify order and performance data
Using approved Shopify API scopes (for example `read_orders`), we may process:
- Order totals and timestamps
- Discount totals
- Refund amounts
- Aggregate performance metrics and trend snapshots

### 3.3 Derived analytics
- Weekly metric snapshots
- Alert records generated by ProfitGuard rules
- Operational logs related to scheduled runs and app reliability

## 4. How we use information

We use collected information to:
- Provide dashboard metrics and alerts inside ProfitGuard
- Run scheduled weekly snapshot processing
- Support billing and plan-limit behavior
- Monitor app reliability, troubleshoot issues, and improve product performance
- Respond to support requests

## 5. Legal basis and merchant responsibility

If required by applicable law, merchants are responsible for ensuring they have a valid legal basis for processing personal data through Shopify and third-party apps they install, including ProfitGuard.

## 5a. International Compliance
ProfitGuard is designed for global use. We aim to comply with major privacy regulations, including:
- GDPR (Europe): Data subjects have rights to access, correct, delete, and export their data. We process data only as needed for app functionality and do not store customer personal data.
- CCPA (California): California residents may request access to or deletion of their data. We do not sell data.
- Other regional laws: We will update our policy as needed to comply with new regulations.

For any region-specific requests, contact: support@zestorax.com

## 6. Data sharing and disclosures

We do not sell merchant or customer data. We may share information only as needed to:
- Operate the service with trusted infrastructure providers (hosting, database, monitoring)
- Comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, or enforce our terms
- Protect rights, security, and integrity of ProfitGuard and its users

## 7. Data retention

We retain data only as long as needed for legitimate business purposes, legal obligations, and service continuity.

- **Session data** is deleted immediately when the app is uninstalled.
- **Shop data** (metrics, alerts, billing records) is permanently deleted within **30 days** of receiving the GDPR shop/redact webhook from Shopify.
- You may request immediate deletion at any time by contacting **support@zestorax.com**.

## 8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Merchant rights and data requests

Depending on applicable law, merchants may request access, correction, deletion, or export of data related to their use of ProfitGuard.

For requests, contact: **support@zestorax.com**

## 10. App uninstall and deletion workflow

When the app is uninstalled:
- **Session data** is deleted immediately.
- Shopify sends a `shop/redact` GDPR webhook 48 hours after uninstall. Upon receiving it, all remaining shop data (metrics, alerts, billing records, email preferences) is **permanently deleted within 30 days**.
- You may request immediate deletion by contacting **support@zestorax.com**.

## 11. International transfers

If data is transferred across jurisdictions, we will use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

## 12. Children

ProfitGuard is a business application and is not directed to children.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date.

## 14. Contact

For privacy-related questions or region-specific requests, contact: **support@zestorax.com**
